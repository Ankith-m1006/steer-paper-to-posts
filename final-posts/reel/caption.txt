When taking GLP-1 medicines, protecting your muscle matters alongside losing weight. In the STEP-1 trial, participants taking the medicine lost an average of 17.32 kg compared to 2.65 kg in placebo, and 40% of total body weight loss was attributable to a reduction in lean mass. Research suggests these declines may relate to reduced calorie intake, inadequate macro- or micronutrient intake, and low physical activity.
