## Carousel: McLaren et al., Nat Metab 2024

- **Paper:** Weight gain leads to greater adverse metabolic responses in South Asian compared with white European men: the GlasVEGAS study.
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1038/s42255-024-01101-z · licence: cc by
- **Key message:** South Asian biology responds differently to weight gain, showing sharper drops in insulin sensitivity, which is why metabolic care must be personalized and doctor-led.
- **Why this angle:** This message addresses the audience's frustration with standard diet advice by grounding their experience in biology rather than personal failure. By highlighting metabolic differences documented in clinical research, it reinforces the need for personalized doctor-led plans built on blood work.
