Paper in, posts out.

A research paper goes in. A carousel, a static post, English and Hindi reels and captions come out, in Steer's voice, with every line pointing to the sentence and page of the paper it came from. A Steer doctor approves on one page before anything is scheduled.

1 · IngestPDF or link, split by page or paragraph
2 · Extractclaims with verbatim quotes; code drops any quote or number not in the paper
3 · WriteSteer's voice; every line cites claims; numbers, drug names and promises checked
4 · Judgea doctor-style pass per line; one rewrite; leftovers flagged
5 · Createbrand renders, AI voiceover, motion, subtitles
6 · Review gatedoctor approves line by line; only approved posts are scheduled

Is weight regain inevitable after stopping weight loss medicine? Research shows that one year after stopping once-weekly subcutaneous GLP-1 medicine 2.4 mg and lifestyle intervention, participants regained two-thirds of their prior weight loss. From week 0 to week 68, mean weight loss was 17.3% with the medicine, but after stopping, net loss dropped to 5.6% by week 120.

At Steer, we focus on doctor-led care, thorough screening, and long-term habit support. You need a long-term plan made with your doctor, including what happens when the medicine stops.
