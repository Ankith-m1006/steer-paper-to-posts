Caption

If you are considering GLP-1 medicines, muscle loss is a fair worry. In clinical trials, participants lost 10% or more of muscle mass over 68- to 72-week interventions. This was linked to reduced calorie intake, low protein, and low activity. Guidelines recommend 1.0–1.5 g/kg daily protein intake and muscle-strengthening exercise on two or more days per week.
